| Feedback condition | Solves | `pass@1` | `pass@3` | `pass^3` | Mean actions | Mean elapsed time |

`pass@3` means at least one success in the three attempts; `pass^3` means all three succeeded. The model called the source tool correctly in all six runs. It nevertheless failed to derive or test the working traversal path, used all six actions in every run, and sometimes claimed success without the evaluator observing the flag. Every run ended with `tool_call_limit`.

## Interpretation

This matched calibration found no solves and no success-rate difference between raw and structured feedback. It shows a floor effect for this model/task/prompt combination; it does **not** establish that the harnesses are equivalent or that either is generally reliable. Do not use these six runs as the main experiment without first addressing model/task feasibility.
